Chroniko: live activity

A made-up feed of deploys, jobs, errors and alerts, streamed from /events on npm start’s server (or made up in this page when that isn’t there). The red line is now, and new events flash as they arrive. Deploys, jobs and some alerts are bars that grow while they run, and stop when they finish. The view follows now while the ● Live button is lit; zooming keeps following, and dragging, the arrow keys or Fit stop it. Press ● Live to go back. Events that ended more than 30 minutes ago are dropped.
